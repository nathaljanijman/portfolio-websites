## 🎯 Problem Statement

### The Pain Points

**For Professionals (Consultants, Product Owners, Freelancers):**
- ❌ Generic LinkedIn profiles don't convert to clients
- ❌ DIY websites (Wix, Squarespace) look unprofessional
- ❌ Agency websites cost €10K+ and take months
- ❌ No clear way to capture leads effectively
- ❌ SEO is a mystery - they don't get found on Google

**For Athletes (Voetbalsters, Sporters):**
- ❌ Instagram is niet genoeg voor sponsorships
- ❌ Geen professionele plek om prestaties te tonen
- ❌ Media/scouts kunnen achievements niet vinden
- ❌ Presskit is vaak een Word document (niet professioneel)
- ❌ Geen controle over hun online narrative

### What Exists Today (Competition)

| Solution | Problem |
|----------|---------|
| **DIY (Wix, Squarespace)** | Ziet er template-achtig uit, geen SEO, geen lead optimization |
| **Agencies (€10K+)** | Te duur, lange doorlooptijd (3-6 maanden), overkill features |
| **Freelance developers** | Inconsistente kwaliteit, vaak technisch maar niet marketing-minded |
| **MRboost (concurrent)** | Goede service, maar weinig focus op lead generation & analytics |

### Our Solution

**High-quality, lead-focused websites in 1-8 weeks for €350-€3.500.**

Why we win:
- ✅ **Sneller** - Weken ipv maanden (design approval gates)
- ✅ **Goedkoper** - Transparante pricing, geen verrassingen
- ✅ **Lead-focused** - Niet alleen mooi, maar converteert
- ✅ **Modern tech** - Next.js, headless CMS, top performance
- ✅ **Personal touch** - Direct contact met Nathalja, geen tussenpersonen

## 🎨 Template Strategy

### Template Philosophy

**NOT:** 100 verschillende templates waar klanten uit kiezen
**YES:** 2-3 basis templates die volledig customizable zijn

**Why:**
- Sneller te bouwen (shared codebase)
- Makkelijker te onderhouden (updates in 1 plek)
- Toch uniek per klant (kleuren, content, foto's, branding)

## ⚠️ Risks & Mitigations

### Risk 1: Scope Creep

**Probability:** HIGH
**Impact:** MEDIUM (delays, unprofitable projects)

**Risk:**
- Client keeps asking for "one more feature"
- Unlimited revisions = endless changes
- Project drags on, margin erodes

**Mitigation:**
- ✅ Clear package definitions in contract
- ✅ "Onbeperkte revisies" = time-boxed (4 weeks All-In, 8 weeks Premium)
- ✅ Change request process (anything out of scope = new quote)
- ✅ Design approval gate (lock design before development)

---

### Risk 2: Client Non-Response

**Probability:** MEDIUM
**Impact:** MEDIUM (project delays, cash flow)

**Risk:**
- Client doesn't send content on time
- Doesn't respond to design feedback
- Project stalls for weeks/months

**Mitigation:**
- ✅ Clear deadlines in onboarding (content due by Day X)
- ✅ Friendly reminders (email, WhatsApp)
- ✅ Escalation protocol (phone call, project on-hold after 2 weeks)
- ✅ Contract clause: "Project paused after 30 days non-response, resumes when client ready"

---

### Risk 3: Technical Debt

**Probability:** MEDIUM
**Impact:** HIGH (maintenance nightmare, hard to scale)

**Risk:**
- Building custom code per client = hard to maintain
- Tech stack changes, old sites break
- Security vulnerabilities

**Mitigation:**
- ✅ Shared template codebase (not custom per client)
- ✅ Regular updates (Next.js, Payload, dependencies)
- ✅ Automated testing (CI/CD pipeline)
- ✅ Onderhoud add-on covers update costs

---

### Risk 4: Competition

**Probability:** HIGH
**Impact:** MEDIUM (pricing pressure, differentiation)

**Risk:**
- Many freelancers/agencies offer websites
- AI tools (Wix AI, v0.dev) getting better
- Price wars (race to bottom)

**Mitigation:**
- ✅ **Differentiation:** Lead generation focus (not just design)
- ✅ **Quality:** Modern tech stack, performance, SEO
- ✅ **Speed:** Deliver in weeks, not months
- ✅ **Service:** Personal touch, training, support
- ✅ **Recurring revenue:** Add-ons reduce reliance on new clients

---

### Risk 5: Cashflow (Delayed Payments)

**Probability:** LOW
**Impact:** HIGH (can't pay bills)

**Risk:**
- Client doesn't pay 2nd installment
- Waiting weeks/months for payment
- Can't fund operations

**Mitigation:**
- ✅ 50% upfront (covers costs, reduces risk)
- ✅ No go-live without final payment (leverage)
- ✅ Clear payment terms (netto 14 dagen)
- ✅ Automated reminders (Mollie/Stripe)
- ✅ Emergency fund (3 months expenses)
